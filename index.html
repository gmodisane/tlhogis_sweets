Good to know

Things worth knowing

How far in advance should I order?

Two days at the very least. Bigger cakes need more, and December fills up fast, so message me early if you can.

How do I pay?

No cash, digital payments only. Half up front to book the date, the rest before the cake leaves. I'll send my banking details on WhatsApp once we've agreed on the order.

Can I send a photo of the cake I want?

Please do, it makes my life easier. A picture, a colour, a theme, a soccer team, anything. I'll tell you what I can do with it.

Can I order online?

Not yet. WhatsApp for now. Maybe one day.

What about allergies and dietary needs?

This is a home kitchen, and wheat, eggs, dairy and nuts are in it every week, so I can't promise a cake hasn't touched any of them. Tell me about an allergy when you message and I'll be straight with you about what I can do.

What if I need to change or cancel?

Tell me as early as you can. Before I start baking, changes are usually fine. Once the shopping is done and the cake is underway, the deposit covers that.

So, what are we baking?

Send me the date and what it's for. I usually answer the same day. Just don't leave it until the night before.
